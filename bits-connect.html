How it works

One secure doorway between your users and your servers

Bits Connect installs on a Windows server in your office. It becomes the only entry point: users sign in on a web page, and Bits Connect opens the application on your server and streams just the picture to their browser.

Requirements

What you need

Gateway server

  • Windows Server 2019 or newer (Windows 10/11 Pro for small or single-user setups)
  • 4 GB RAM or more and 2 GB free disk for Bits Connect
  • A fixed IP in the office and port 443 forwarded on your router for internet access
  • Your own domain name and HTTPS certificate recommended (for example apps.yourcompany.in)

Users

  • Any up-to-date browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari
  • Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iPhone or iPad
  • A normal internet connection; 2 Mbps is comfortable for office applications
  • No software, plug-in or VPN to install

About Microsoft licences. For several people to use apps on one server at the same time, Microsoft requires Windows Server with Remote Desktop Services client access licences (RDS CALs), bought from Microsoft or an authorised distributor. Bits Connect works with your RDS licences; it does not replace them. Office PC mode, where each person uses their own Windows 10/11 Pro PC, needs no RDS licences.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do my users need to install anything?

No. They open the web address you give them, sign in, and click the app. It works the same on a laptop, phone or tablet.

How is this safer than opening Remote Desktop to the internet?

Exposed Remote Desktop ports are among the most attacked services on the internet. With Bits Connect the port stays closed. Users reach only a secure web page that checks their password, a second sign-in step, their country and allowed hours, and blocks repeated attempts.

Will my existing software work?

Any Windows application that runs on your server can be published, including Tally, Busy, Marg, ERP and CRM packages, billing and clinic systems, and in-house programs.

Can users print and save files?

Yes. Printing produces a PDF on the user's own device, and files can be uploaded and downloaded. You decide per group whether printing, upload, download and copy-paste are allowed.

Can I use my own web address and logo?

Yes. Point a name such as apps.yourcompany.in at your office and install your certificate. The Web and Enterprise editions show your logo and company details on the sign-in page.

What happens to our data?

It stays on your own server in your office. Bits Connect does not copy your data to any cloud; it only streams the screen image to the signed-in user.

Who installs and supports it?

Bestow IT Service handles installation, training and ongoing support. Contact us for a demo and a quote for your number of users.
