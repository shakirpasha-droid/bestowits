What it does

Cheque printing made simple, accurate and accountable

Choose the bank account, type the payee and amount, and Bits Cheque Print fills in the cheque leaf exactly in the right places, to the CTS-2010 cheque standard used by Indian banks. The amount in words is written for you in Lakh and Crore. Every cheque is saved to a searchable register, so you always know what was issued, to whom, and when.

Printed on the cheque

  • Date
  • Payee name
  • Amount in words
  • Amount in figures
  • A/C Payee crossing (optional)

Only these items are printed. Signatures and anything else on the leaf stay with you.

Questions

Frequently asked

Will it work with my bank's cheque?

Yes, if it is a CTS-2010 cheque, which all Indian banks now use. 35 ready layouts are included, and the layout designer handles any other design.

What if my bank changes its cheque design?

Import a scan of the new cheque in the layout designer and move the items into place. No need to wait for an update.

Is my data safe?

Your data stays on your own PC or office server, with automatic daily backups. Users log in with their own passwords.

Can several people use it at once?

Yes. Set one PC as the server and connect other office PCs to it. Each allowed PC uses one licence seat.
