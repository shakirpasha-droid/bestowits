How a patient moves through Bits Lab

One screen for every desk, from the counter to the pathologist.

Register

New or returning patient found by mobile number. Token issued for the waiting hall.

Bill

Tests and packages from your rate list, discounts with approver, split cash / UPI / card.

Collect

Barcode labels for each sample. Preparation instructions sent to the patient.

Process

Sample tracking by stage, worklist for the lab, outside-lab tests sent and received.

Result & verify

Ranges, critical-value alerts, previous results, templates. Doctor verifies and signs.

Hand over

Front desk checks the balance due, then prints, saves as PDF or sends on WhatsApp.

Backup

Your lab's data, backed up on your PC and on Google Drive

Bits Lab backs up every day by itself. Keep one copy on the server PC and a second copy on a USB drive or in your Google Drive, so a failed hard disk never costs you a day's work.

Local backup

On the server PC, with no setup.

  • Full backup every day and every time the server closes
  • The last 30 daily backups are kept automatically
  • "Back up now" button for an extra copy at any time
  • Second copy to a USB drive or another disk
  • A backup is saved before every software update
  • Restore any backup, with a preview of what it contains first

Google Drive backup

Off-site copy in your own Google account.

  • Every backup is copied to your Google Drive automatically
  • Uses the free Google Drive for desktop app on the server PC
  • Bits Lab finds your Google Drive folder by itself; choose it with one click
  • Works the same with OneDrive, Dropbox, MEGA and pCloud folders
  • Backups go only to your account, never to Bestow's servers
  • The server panel shows the time of the last copy, or why it failed

FAQ

Common questions

Can we bring our existing patients, doctors and test rates?

Yes. Fill the Bits Lab Excel template, or export from your old software to Excel, and import patients, referring doctors and tests with rates. Duplicates are skipped.

Does Bits Lab back up to Google Drive?

Yes. Bits Lab saves a full backup on the server PC every day and copies each one to your Google Drive folder. Install the free Google Drive for desktop app on the server PC, then pick its folder in the Bits Lab server panel. A USB drive or OneDrive folder works the same way.

What happens if the internet goes down?

Bits Lab runs on your own network, so billing, sample tracking and reports keep working. The licence only needs to check in again within the grace period.

Will updates delete our data?

No. Your data is kept outside the program folder, and every new version saves a full backup before it starts. Installing an update never removes records.

Can we add new tests or change our letterhead ourselves?

Yes. Departments, tests, rates, parameters and reference ranges are edited in Settings. Bills and reports can use a coloured letterhead or your pre-printed stationery.

We forgot the admin password. Is the data lost?

No. Use the owner recovery code given at setup, or call Bestow for a one-time recovery token for your PC. It only resets the administrator password.

Can reports be sent to patients on WhatsApp?

Yes, from the front desk after the balance is checked. Bills and preparation instructions can also be sent on WhatsApp.
