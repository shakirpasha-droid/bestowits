What it does

MedFit Bits follows each candidate from the front desk to the final result, and shows every department what is waiting for it.

  1. RegistrationDetails and Wafid slip number entered, photo taken and fingerprint enrolled. The candidate gets a number, for example 021.
  2. DepartmentsMedical Exam, Sample Collection, X-Ray and Vaccination. Staff verify the candidate by fingerprint before entering results.
  3. PathologyResults entered against normal ranges: out-of-range values show in red, normal values in green.
  4. Final ReviewThe doctor sees all results and X-ray images, approves or rejects each image, and gives FIT or UNFIT.
  5. Medical reportPrinted or saved as PDF, with the clinic's letterhead.

Features

Everything a GCC medical centre does in a day, in one system.

Registration and identity

  • Passport, nationality, date of birth, destination country and Wafid slip number
  • Candidate photo from a webcam
  • Fingerprint enrolment and verification (SecuGen scanners)
  • Candidate Registry with green and amber progress lamps for every department

Departments

  • Medical Exam: history and clinical examination
  • Sample Collection: barcode labels for blood and urine samples
  • X-Ray: up to 4 images per candidate in a zoomable viewer
  • Vaccination: vaccination records per candidate
  • Each department sees its own queue; reopening a seen candidate needs a staff fingerprint

Pathology

  • Biochemistry, CBC, serology, urine and stool results
  • Clinic's own normal ranges shown beside each test
  • Out-of-range values red, normal values green
  • Normal answers pre-filled; entries checked against each test's format

Final review and reports

  • All department results in one screen
  • X-ray review: approve, reject or ask for re-import, with comments
  • FIT / UNFIT decision with clear badges
  • Medical report printed or saved as PDF
  • Daily view of the current workday, with search by date

Waiting-room TV display

  • Runs on any Android smart TV
  • Candidate numbers only, never names or results
  • "Now calling" and "Next in queue" per department, automatic or manual
  • Live counters and up to three scrolling notice strips

Control and safety

  • Staff logins by department, with fingerprint sign-in
  • Audit trail of who did what and when
  • Works on the clinic's own network, no internet needed
  • Starts automatically with Windows on server and client PCs

How it runs

One server PC holds the data. Every other PC opens MedFit Bits in Chrome or Edge, with nothing to install except where a fingerprint scanner is attached.

ItemDetails
Server PCWindows 10 or 11, Microsoft SQL Server Express (free), .NET 8
Department PCsAny Windows PC on the same network, using Chrome or Edge
NetworkClinic LAN. Works without internet
FingerprintSecuGen fingerprint scanner on the PCs that verify candidates
CameraUSB webcam at registration
BarcodeTSC TE244 label printer (50 x 25 mm labels) and TVS BS-C101 STAR barcode scanner
PrintingAny printer installed in Windows, plus Save as PDF
Waiting-room TVAny Android smart TV with a web browser, on the clinic network
