3. Activation, check-in and licence status

Activation needs an internet connection once, unless we give you an offline licence. After that the app checks in with the Bits Licence Server regularly, normally once a day.

  • Offline grace. If a PC cannot check in, the app keeps working for the grace period shown in the app (for example 10 days). After that, new work is paused until it checks in again.
  • Status set by Bestowits. We may mark a licence as Warning, Read-only, Suspended or Revoked, for example for unpaid fees, misuse or a breach of these terms. Read-only lets you view and print records but not add new ones. We will tell you the reason and how to fix it.
  • Expiry. When a licence or trial ends, new work (such as new bills) is paused. Your existing records stay on your PC and can still be viewed, printed and backed up.
  • What is sent. Check-ins send only licence details, as listed in our Privacy Policy, never your business or patient records.
