What it does

RentDesk replaces the notebook, the Excel sheets and the loose receipt books. You enter each property and its units once. After that it tells you who owes what, prints the receipts, tracks the deposits and warns you before an agreement expires.

Features

Everything a small landlord or property manager does in a month, in one place.

Properties and units

  • Buildings, floors and units (shops, offices, flats)
  • Rent fixed or per sq ft, with maintenance per sq ft
  • Manage buildings you own or buildings you look after for someone else; the right owner prints on each receipt
  • Vacant and let status at a glance

Tenants and leases

  • Agreement term, start date, rent, maintenance and deposit
  • Yearly rent increase (hike) set for each lease
  • Individual or company tenant, with PAN and phone
  • Alerts for expiring and expired agreements, and a renewal list

Rent ledger and collections

  • Month-by-month rent due, paid and balance for each tenant
  • Payments are applied to the oldest month first; extra money shows as advance
  • Cash, cheque, NEFT/IMPS and UPI entries, with a collections report by mode
  • Cheque status: pending, cleared or bounced; bounced cheques do not count as paid

Deposits

  • Security deposit receipt when the tenant joins
  • Deposit refund and deposit adjustment vouchers
  • Running deposit balance for each lease

Receipts and statements

  • Rent receipts, deposit receipts and refund vouchers on A4
  • Original and duplicate copy on one page
  • Amount in words, for example "Rupees Fourteen Thousand Five Hundred Only"
  • Tenant statements, with your name, address, PAN and note on top

Expenses and profit

  • Electricity, water, watchman and in-charge salaries, lift, generator, repairs and your own expense heads
  • Recurring expenses posted when they fall due
  • Income against expenses report for the month, last 6 months or financial year

Compliance reminders

  • Agreements over 11 months flagged for registration
  • Stamp duty and registration fee estimate when you create a lease
  • TDS reminder when monthly rent crosses ₹50,000, and a prompt to collect the TDS certificate
  • Police verification status for each tenant

Users and permissions

  • Separate logins for you, your manager and your clerk
  • Tick exactly what each person can view, add or edit and delete
  • Limit a person to selected properties
  • Automatic sign-out when the PC is left idle
  • Activity log of who did what

Your data, under your control

  • Backup and restore your data
  • Reset data by part (receipts, expenses, leases, properties) with the Admin password and a 60 second countdown you can cancel
  • Locked out? Use the recovery code from setup, or ask Bestow IT Service for a one-time recovery token made for your PC. There are no hidden accounts.

Built around Indian and Telangana rules

  • Amounts in rupees with lakh and crore, and the April to March financial year
  • 11-month rental agreements on e-stamp paper and the registration rule for longer terms
  • Tenant TDS on rent and PAN on every receipt
  • Reminders are guidance. Check stamp duty and tax with your advocate or CA.

Who it is for

RentDesk suits people who collect rent from a handful to a few dozen units and want it organised without hiring accountants or buying a large property system.

Landlords with commercial buildings

Shops, offices and floors let to businesses, with deposits, yearly hikes and TDS on rent.

Owners of flats and houses

A few residential units let on 11-month agreements, with monthly receipts and renewals.

Small property managers

People who collect rent for other owners and need the right owner on each receipt and clear statements.

Families sharing a property

Give each family member the access they need, and keep a record of every change.

RentDesk is not a listing portal and does not collect rent online. It is the record-keeping and receipt desk behind your rent collection.
