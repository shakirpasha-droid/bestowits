Our IT Services Explained

Desktop & Computer Support in Hyderabad

We provide desktop troubleshooting, upgrades, operating system support, software installation, hardware replacement and performance optimization. Our support covers common boot problems, blue-screen errors, storage issues, memory upgrades, power problems and no-display faults.

Laptop Support & Upgrades

Our laptop support helps resolve power and charging problems, overheating, random shutdowns, display issues, BIOS problems, battery issues, memory and storage requirements. We also assist with operating system installation, software configuration and hardware upgrades.

Computer AMC & Annual IT Maintenance

Our computer AMC services are designed for businesses that want dependable ongoing technical support. Preventive maintenance, system health checks, troubleshooting and routine support can help minimize downtime and keep office computers productive.

Network Installation & Wi-Fi Solutions

We plan and support office networks including CAT6 cabling, racks, patch panels, switches, routers, firewalls and Wi-Fi access points. We also troubleshoot slow networks, connectivity problems, IP conflicts, DHCP issues and wireless coverage problems.

Server & IT Infrastructure Support

We support business server environments including Windows Server, Active Directory, DNS, user permissions, backups, monitoring and virtualization. Our goal is to keep essential business applications and shared resources available and organized.

Data Backup & Recovery Services

We help businesses plan reliable backups and assist with common data-loss situations such as accidental deletion, corrupted files, storage errors and formatted drives. Recovery options depend on the condition of the storage device and the nature of the data loss.

CCTV & Biometric Solutions

We provide installation and technical support for IP CCTV systems and biometric attendance devices. We can assist with camera placement, network connectivity, recording configuration and basic troubleshooting.

Tally & Business IT Solutions

We support business technology environments including Tally solutions, Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, business email, backup and office IT infrastructure. Our focus is on practical solutions that help businesses work efficiently and securely.
