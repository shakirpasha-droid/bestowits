What it does

A patient visit, start to finish

Each step picks up where the last one left off, so nothing is typed twice. A treatment marked done goes onto the bill, takes its materials out of stock and sets the next reminder.

3 · Front desk

Bill and collect

  • GST bills numbered by financial year

4 · After the visit

Bring them back

  • Follow-up list so no patient is forgotten

Backup to Google Drive

Automatic backups, with a safe copy on Google Drive

Bits Dental backs up the full database and every patient file (X-rays, photos and documents) on the schedule you set: daily, weekly or every few hours. Each backup is also copied to your clinic's Google Drive, so a copy of your records stays safe outside the clinic even if the PC is damaged, stolen or hit by a virus.

  1. 1Install Google Drive for desktop on the server PC and sign in with the clinic's Google account.
  2. 2Choose the Google Drive folder for backups in Bits Dental's backup settings.
  3. 3Every backup is copied there automatically, and Google Drive uploads it to the cloud.
  4. 4Restore any copy when you need it, from the PC, a USB drive or Google Drive. A safety copy of the current data is taken first.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do we need internet to use Bits Dental?

No, not for daily work. The clinic's PCs connect to the server PC over your own network. Internet is needed to send SMS and WhatsApp messages and for a periodic licence check. An offline licence is available for clinics without a reliable connection.

Where is our patient data kept?

On the server PC in your clinic, in a Microsoft SQL Server database. Automatic backups go to a folder on that PC, and each one is also copied to your Google Drive and, if you like, a USB drive or network folder.

How does the Google Drive backup work?

Install Google Drive for desktop on the server PC and sign in with the clinic's Google account. In Bits Dental, choose the Google Drive folder for backups. From then on every scheduled backup is copied there and Google Drive uploads it. Your Google password is never entered in Bits Dental. To restore, pick any backup from the PC, a USB drive or Google Drive.

Can we bring in our existing patient records?

Yes. Import patients, the treatment price list, appointments, doctors and stock from Excel or CSV. Bits Dental matches your column headings and lets you check the file before anything is saved. Old file numbers are kept.

Which printers does it work with?

Any printer installed in Windows. Bills print on A4 or A5, receipts on A4 or 80 mm thermal paper, and folder stickers on A4 label sheets. Any printout can also be saved as a PDF to send to the patient.

Can we use it in more than one branch?

Yes. Bits Dental supports several branches in one system, so the owner sees every location in one place.

How do we try it?

Download the free trial from this page and install it on your server PC, or contact Bestow IT Services for a demo. We install it on your server PC, set up your clinic name, logo and price list, and train your staff.
