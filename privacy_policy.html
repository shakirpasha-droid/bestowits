Privacy Policy

Privacy information for Bits desktop software, the Bits Licence Server, the Bestowits website and customer support.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

1. Who we are and what this covers

Bits apps run on your own computers, and the business or patient data you enter stays with you, not with us. We collect only what we need to licence, update and support the software.

This policy is published by Bestow IT Services ("Bestowits", "BITS", "we", "us"), developer of the Bits range of software. BITS is short for Bestow IT Services. It applies to:

  • Bits desktop apps: Bits Lab, Bits Dental, Bits MedFit, Bits Cheque Print, Bits RentDesk, Bits Realty, Bits Shield, Bits Download Manager, Bits Connect, and any other app sold under the Bits name;
  • the Bits Licence Server at licence.bestowits.com;
  • our website www.bestowits.com and our support by phone, email, WhatsApp and remote session.

Where a Bits app is supplied under a client's own brand (for example a clinic's name on screen), that client controls its business data. This policy still applies to Bestowits' part.

We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") and its Rules, and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

3. Data we do not collect

The records you keep in a Bits app stay on your own computers or your own database server. They are not sent to Bestowits. This includes:

  • patients, test results, medical reports, dental charts and images;
  • bills, payments, accounts, cheques, tenants, rent and property details;
  • your staff, doctors, referrers and customers.

For this data, you (the clinic, lab or business using the app) decide what is collected and why. Under the DPDP Act you are the Data Fiduciary, and you are responsible for your own notices and consents to your patients and customers.

We see this data only if you show it to us during a support session or send us a file to fix a problem. We then use it only for that purpose, keep it confidential, and delete any copy once the problem is solved.

4. Cloud backup (Google Drive, OneDrive, MEGA, pCloud)

Cloud backup is optional and goes only to your own cloud account. Backups never pass through Bestowits' servers.

  • How you connect. In the app's Backup & Sync page you sign in to Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive or pCloud on the provider's own sign-in page and approve access. For MEGA you enter your MEGA login in the app, which keeps only a session, not your password.
  • What the app can reach. For Google Drive, only the files and folders the app itself creates (the drive.file permission). For OneDrive, only the app's own folder (Apps › Bits). The app does not read, change or delete any other file in your cloud.
  • What is uploaded. Backup files made by the app, encrypted with your backup password before they leave the PC. Without that password or your recovery key, neither Bestowits nor the cloud provider can open them.
  • Where the sign-in is kept. On your PC only, protected by Windows. Bestowits never receives your cloud password or access tokens.
  • Stopping. Click Disconnect in the app, or remove the app's access in your Google, Microsoft, MEGA or pCloud account settings. Backups already in your cloud stay there until you delete them.
  • The provider's own terms. Your cloud provider handles the stored files under its own privacy policy and terms.

Google user data. Bits apps' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google Drive access only to save, list, restore and remove your app's backup files. We do not use it for advertising, do not sell it, do not let people read it, and do not use it to train AI models.

5. How we share, store, protect and keep data

Sharing. We share personal data only with:

  • service providers who run our systems for us, under confidentiality terms: Cloudflare (hosts the Bits Licence Server), and our email, messaging and accounting providers;
  • authorised resellers or partners who sold or support your licence, limited to your licence details;
  • government or courts when Indian law requires it.

Where it is stored. Licence records are held on Cloudflare's network, which may process them outside India. We transfer data abroad only as the DPDP Act allows.

Protection. Licence data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Licences are digitally signed, so they cannot be forged or edited. Admin access is limited to Bestowits staff, protected by a secret token and a PIN, and every change is logged.

6. Your rights and how to reach us

You can ask us, about the personal data Bestowits holds on you:

  • for a summary of what we hold and who we shared it with;
  • to correct, complete or update it;
  • to delete it, where we no longer need it and the law does not require us to keep it;
  • to withdraw consent you gave; this stops future use but may mean we cannot keep supporting a licence;
  • to name someone to act for you if you die or cannot act yourself.
