How it works

From an ordinary desktop PC to a working NAS in under an hour.

Install

Boot the PC from the BitsVault USB stick and install it on a small SSD. Your data drives are not touched.

Set up

Open the address shown on the NAS screen in any browser and create the administrator account.

Add storage

Create a storage pool from your hard drives, then add shared folders and users.

Use it

Open the folders from Windows, Mac or phone. BitsVault watches the drives and runs backups for you.

Frequently asked questions

 

Do I need a special NAS box?

No. BitsVault runs on an ordinary desktop PC. Add a small SSD for the system and hard drives for your data.

Can Windows and Mac computers use it at the same time?

Yes. Shared folders open directly in Windows File Explorer and Mac Finder, and phones connect with any SMB file app.

What happens if a hard drive fails?

With a mirror pool your files stay available. BitsVault alerts you, you replace the drive and add it back to the pool.

Can I reach my files from outside the office?

Yes, through a private Tailscale VPN. Your NAS is never exposed to the open internet.

What if the system SSD fails or I reinstall?

Your files are on the data drives, not the SSD. After reinstalling, BitsVault imports the existing pools with your files and versions intact.

Is my data sent to any cloud?

No. Your files stay on your own NAS. Internet is used only for licence activation, alerts and optional remote access.

Is there a mobile app?

A BitsVault Android app is coming soon. Until then, phones and tablets can open shared folders with any SMB file manager app.
